## Evidence and limits

Reviewed all proposed closures, successors and exclusions against source notes, location identity, and available independent sources. Cross-checked successor permits against the directly downloaded Metro establishment/inspection records and scanned all original places for existing successor pins. Also reviewed a targeted open sample across chain and independent files (Dairy Queen, Panera, Zaxby’s, Jersey Mike’s, Fazoli’s, Bob Evans, Dairy Kastle, Frascelli’s, Highlands Tap Room, Wiltshire, Double Dragon, Happy China, Cast Iron, Jade Palace, Cottage Cafe and Sam’s Gyros).

Current active permits plus recent 2026 inspections are positive evidence of recent operation, not a promise of today’s hours. The separate permitted-food endpoint omits 941 food-service records that exist in the inspection table; absence alone was never used as closure proof. Some official geocodes are zero, off-county, or hundreds of meters wrong. Census geocoding is approximate street interpolation and establishes location only, not operation. Mall interior pins remain approximate even where the mall itself confirms the tenant. Seasonal venues do not promise winter/daily service.

Some original URLs now redirect or return bot/payment errors. Those failures were not treated as closure. For example, the Allison Lane Showcase listing now redirects, so the accepted records add a working September 2026 broker listing and a separate permanent-closure listing. Search-visible source content was used where full pages were blocked. No ownership conclusions were inferred from operating-status evidence.
