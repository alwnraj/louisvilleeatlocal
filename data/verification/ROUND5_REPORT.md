## Resolutions

- **Moby Dick, 12414 Dixie → Domino’s, 12416 Dixie.** A newly found transcription of completed April 2025 permit COM-ELC-25-00678 explicitly connects the old Moby Dick premises to the Domino’s conversion. Current own restaurant hours and a November 2026 inspection at the mapped building resolve the two-number address discrepancy. No existing Domino’s pin duplicates it. The former business was Louisville Moby Dick Seafood, not the unrelated House of Kabob chain.
- **Queenie’s, 2956 Richland → Lemongrass Café & Market.** An independent 2011 account of eating at Queenie’s establishes its exact historical address. Current own ordering/hours and a recent inspected active permit establish Lemongrass at the same premises, within about 7m of the pin. No duplicate Lemongrass pin exists.
- **Wendy’s, 2100 South Floyd: closed.** UofL’s own sustainability page explicitly states that SAC Wendy’s closed in 2025, resolving conflict with undated campus directories still listing the old tenant.
- **Homemade Ice Cream and Pie Kitchen, 1041 Bardstown: closed, medium confidence.** Exact-address listing explicitly says permanently closed, and the own current location directory lists other Bardstown branches. The contrary generic opening-hours page admits the hours are not venue-confirmed; it does not establish reopening.
- **Whirlaway Tavern, 2532 South Fourth: closed.** The building owner’s March 2020 release explicitly identifies the former tavern and its conversion into LongTail entrepreneurial/event space. The owner’s company profile describes the same use. Do not attach Chicago’s same-name bar or add private event/office space as a public restaurant.
- **5 O’Clock Charlies, 14808 Taylorsville: outside scope, medium confidence.** Independent exact-address liquor-store and convenience-retail directories agree on name, address and phone. Apply the documented liquor-store exclusion. This is a classification finding, not a claim that the shop closed.
