# Missing independent batches and missing-chain cross-check

Checked November 18, 2026. All 315 input places in ind_02, ind_06, ind_07, ind_08 and ind_09 received individual web searches. Results preserve input order. Non-unsure verdicts cite URLs actually returned in searches; records with incomplete address matches, conflicting current evidence, old directories only, or temporary closures remain unsure. A current crawl date alone was not treated as proof of operation.

First-pass independent totals: 176 open, 101 unsure, 32 closed, 2 renamed, 4 duplicate/nonrestaurant. These are preliminary, before parent-agent cross-check corrections. In particular, the initial Akiko closure was rescinded; parent recheck must override it. Against the Grain Highlands closure date in a directory was wrong; parent found September 2024 evidence. Future closure announcements for Vincenzo's and Mile Wide both name December 31, 2026, so neither was marked closed on November 18.

The 68 non-open results from missing chain batches were individually re-searched in results/recheck_missing_chains.json. Different sources were used where available; notes explicitly identify cases that only reconfirm the first source. New unresolved and closure findings need parent merge judgment. Source disagreement does not automatically establish either current operation or permanent closure.

Observed traps: Skyline Chili at 7105 Dixie Highway is in Fairfield, Ohio, not Louisville. Yamato search results for New Albany, Mississippi were rejected for Indiana. Chicago Steak & Lemonade Galene Drive closure does not apply to West Broadway. Several delivery platforms retain menus or say only that platform service ended; those statements alone were not treated as restaurant closures. Some map directories explicitly copy OSM and provide no independent verification. One hours directory says its hours are typical guesses; those were rejected. Moby Dick House of Kabob is a DC-region brand and must not be confused with Louisville Moby Dick Seafood.

Only verification data and this note were written. No application/build, Git, or site changes.
