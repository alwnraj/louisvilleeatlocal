How this was made

The three groups

  • PE-backed: a private equity, growth equity or investment firm owns the business or a significant stake in it. That includes credit funds and former lenders that took a chain over, like Red Lobster (Fortress) or Rally's.
  • Corporate: owned by a public company, or by a large private company or family based outside the Louisville area, such as McDonald's, Chick-fil-A or Darden.
  • Independent: owned by its founders, a family or a local operator, with no private equity or corporate parent found. Louisville-born groups with a few locations, like Heine Brothers' or Bearno's, count as independent.

Where the data comes from

Locations: OpenStreetMap, exported 17 November 2026 for Louisville, Jefferson County and the nearby parts of Southern Indiana, Bullitt and Oldham counties. Stadium and arena stalls, generic labels and liquor stores were removed, and O'Charley's was dropped after its corporate restaurants closed in September 2026.

Operating status: places were checked against official location pages, restaurant websites, local news, public inspection records and other listings on 17–19 November 2026. Places confirmed closed or duplicated and entries outside the public restaurant, café and bar scope were removed; confirmed successors appear under their current name. Places listed as open had supporting evidence on their checked date; this is not a guarantee they are open today. Unresolved places remain visible with an “Operating status not confirmed” flag. Seasonal venues may have limited hours. Operating status and ownership were researched separately.

Ownership: each chain and restaurant group was looked up in news coverage, company announcements, filings and Wikipedia, and its card links the source used. Where reports conflicted, or we couldn't find the current owner, the card says “not confirmed” and the place is not counted as PE-backed.

Independents marked “Confirmed independent” have had their ownership checked. Either we found a source naming a local owner (a family, founder or Louisville restaurant group), or they belong to the Louisville Independent Business Alliance (keeplouisvilleweird.com). The alliance requires majority ownership by Louisville-area residents, a local headquarters, private ownership and no franchise. We also checked every remaining name against OpenStreetMap's Name Suggestion Index of chain brands and against hotel locations; that turned up hotel restaurants, now listed with their hotel's owner, but no chains. The rest are single-location places listed as independent that haven't each been researched; their cards say so.

Caveats

Ownership changes fast. Inspire Brands (Arby's, Dunkin', Sonic and more) and Panera Brands have both filed confidentially for IPOs. Franchised chains are mostly run by local franchisee companies, but the brand, menu and profits flow to the owner shown here.
